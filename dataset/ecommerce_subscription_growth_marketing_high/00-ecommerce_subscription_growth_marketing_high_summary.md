VerdantBox is a fictional DTC subscription wellness-food company headquartered in Seattle, serving customers across the US and Canada, specializing in organic, plant-based, and functional foods, with its flagship membership product, VerdantBox+; the dataset includes 16 tables and roughly 9,000 rows, built around core entities such as customer, campaign, order, marketing_touchpoint, and membership_subscription, paired with 20 SQL queries covering CTEs, window functions, and self-joins; it supports analysis of marketing-mix P&L and budget rebalancing, channel ROAS, membership economics (AOV, LTV), marketing funnel diagnostics, cohort retention, and trial-to-paid conversion; the data spans about 1,000 customers, 500 SKUs, and 50 marketing campaigns.
