Lakeshore Cinemas is a Chicago-headquartered, Midwest regional cinema exhibition chain operating 18 theaters and 92 screens; the dataset includes 10 tables (core entities such as theater, screen, film_booking, showtime, concession_sale, and loyalty_redemption) and supports 20 SQL queries covering aggregation, joins, CTEs, and window functions; it's designed to test whether reported concession gross margin is inflated by Lakeshore Rewards member redemptions, whether losses on secondary-market IMAX screens are masked by overall chain profitability, and whether late_night attendance is padded by buyback tickets under minimum attendance clauses, while also supporting screen utilization and per-showtime concession attach rate analysis; the data covers roughly 38,000 showtimes and 7,500 active members, totaling about 97,000 rows.
