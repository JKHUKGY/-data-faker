StreamCast Media is a fictional US programmatic podcast advertising platform (SSP) that matches advertisers with podcast hosts through real-time bidding (RTB) and second-price auctions, taking a 30% commission; the dataset contains 13 tables covering core entities such as podcast, ad_campaign, listener, ad_impression, ad_auction_log, and revenue_settlement, and supports 27 SQL queries spanning revenue optimization, advertiser ROI, host monetization, listener experience, and auction market dynamics; it can be used to analyze business questions such as whether mid-roll floor prices are underpriced, fill rate optimization during off-peak hours, the payoff from audience-targeting premiums, ad fatigue thresholds, and the correctness of second-price auction billing; the data spans the most recent 60 days, with about 50 podcast shows, 201 episodes, 40 advertisers, 5,000 listeners, 18,000 ad impressions, and 87,000 bid logs, totaling roughly 138,000 rows.
