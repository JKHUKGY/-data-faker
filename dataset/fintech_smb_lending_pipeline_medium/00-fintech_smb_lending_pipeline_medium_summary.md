Pacific Bridge Lending is a mid-sized fintech non-bank lender headquartered in Oakland, California, focused on term loans for small and medium-sized businesses (SMB) across California and earning revenue through interest spread; the dataset includes 10 tables (industry, risk_grade, customer, application, loan, repayment_schedule, payment, default_event, and others) built around roughly 800 customers, about 3,000 applications, and around 2,230 loans, paired with 20 SQL queries that support aggregation, CTE, and window-function analysis for pricing alignment, portfolio concentration, approval leakage, early warning signals, and customer lifetime value; it can be used to investigate whether Grade C loans are underpriced, assess concentration risk in the restaurant and hospitality industry, uncover high-quality customers who were wrongly rejected, catch early repayment-deterioration signals before default, and compare repeat versus new customer performance; the dataset totals roughly 110,000 rows spanning about 22 months of application activity.
