This dataset portrays the underwriting operations of DingAn Commercial Insurance (鼎安商业保险), a mid-sized domestic commercial property and casualty (P&C) insurer, covering 1,000 insured businesses, 3,126 commercial policies, 40 underwriters, 25 claims adjusters, and 60 partner brokers across 24 tables totaling roughly 116,000 rows; it supports both Dashboard and business-question SQL queries for scenarios like renewal decisions, claims and fraud-signal detection, and risk-based pricing, and can be used to check whether risk scores line up with actual loss ratios, decide whether a customer should be re-priced, declined, or renewed, audit the accuracy of past renewal decisions, spot fraud and dispute signals in claims correspondence, and flag renewal risk by combining payment behavior, regulatory penalties, and credit ratings — serving as the cold-start training and validation data for the InsightUnderwriter AI Agent project.
