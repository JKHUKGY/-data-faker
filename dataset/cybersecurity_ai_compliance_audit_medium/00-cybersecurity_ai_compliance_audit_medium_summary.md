Sentinel AI Governance is a fictional B2B SaaS company headquartered in Boston, founded in 2021, that sells an AI compliance audit and governance platform to mid-size and large enterprises, serving roughly 80 tenants with ~$28M ARR; this dataset is a snapshot of a single customer tenant, containing 11 tables with about 1,400 rows covering 30 users, 50 AI models, 100 controls across 6 compliance frameworks, and the accompanying risk assessments, security incidents, and remediation records, supporting 20 aggregation, join, CTE, and window function queries; it can be used to examine business questions such as high-risk model exposure, framework Coverage Rate and Compliance Rate, incident MTTR, overdue remediation, and owner risk concentration.
