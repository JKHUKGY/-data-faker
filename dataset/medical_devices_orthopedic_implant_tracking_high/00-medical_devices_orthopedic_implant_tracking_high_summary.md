This dataset models the central orthopedic implant registry for MAOHN (MidAtlantic Orthopedic Health Network), a fictional nonprofit hospital system spanning 12 acute-care hospitals, with five upstream systems — Epic EHR, SIS, Workday ERP, the FDA GUDID feed, and the FDA enforcement RSS — feeding into this analytical mart every night; its 20 tables (roughly 14,000 rows) are organized around core entities like patient, surgery, inventory (including UDI-DI/UDI-PI), and recall, and support 20 SQL queries covering aggregation, multi-table joins, window functions, and CTEs; it's built to answer business questions such as tracking SLA notifications for Class I/II/III recalls, costing individual implants under point-in-time contract pricing, auditing GPO contract discount compliance, comparing manufacturer and surgeon adverse-event and 2-year revision rates, and analyzing inventory turnover and tied-up capital.
