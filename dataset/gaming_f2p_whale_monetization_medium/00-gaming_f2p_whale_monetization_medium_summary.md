Pinnacle Peak Games is a fictional Seattle-based mobile game studio whose F2P collection RPG, Ember Realms Saga, has been live for eighteen months and racked up over eight million downloads. The dataset spans twelve tables and roughly seventy thousand rows, covering five thousand sampled players, about 17,700 in-app purchase transactions, eight gacha pools, and thirty Live-Ops events, and supports twenty SQL analysis questions. It's built to surface monetization health issues such as reliance on whale revenue, gaps between disclosed and actual gacha probabilities, event stacking that erodes retention, third-party top-up and discount-code arbitrage, and device fingerprint fraud rings.
