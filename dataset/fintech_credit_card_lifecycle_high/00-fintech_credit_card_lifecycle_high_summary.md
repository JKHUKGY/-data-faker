This dataset portrays the full business lifecycle of Keystone Card Company, a California consumer credit card issuer, spanning 16 tables and roughly 194,000 rows (about 8,900 accounts, 89,000 monthly statement rows, 31,000 sampled transactions, and 20,000 prescreen offers), supporting end-to-end SQL analysis across the acquisition funnel, underwriting decisions, statement repayment, and charge-off recovery, and it can answer eight core business questions covering underwriting model calibration, channel adverse selection, transactor profitability, the promo APR cliff, CLI adverse selection, bonus churner negative LTV, vintage deterioration, and product-tier P&L.
