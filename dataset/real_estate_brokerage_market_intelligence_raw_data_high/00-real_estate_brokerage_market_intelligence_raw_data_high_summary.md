This is a raw market intelligence dataset for Crestline Realty, a fictional North American residential real estate brokerage headquartered in San Francisco with roughly 200 agents across 8 offices covering three regional markets — Bay Area, SoCal, and PNW; it contains 23 tables and about 328,000 rows spanning three data domains — MLS listings and closed sales, a Salesforce-style CRM sales pipeline with commission splits, and external macro data from sources like Zillow and Freddie Mac — and supports 20 SQL queries using aggregation, window functions, and cross-domain joins, answering business questions such as whether pricing is reasonable, agent performance attribution, hot/cold market signals, opportunity discovery, commission cash flow forecasting, and customer deduplication/matching; the data spans a roughly 3.4-year window from 2023-01-01 to 2026-06-05.
