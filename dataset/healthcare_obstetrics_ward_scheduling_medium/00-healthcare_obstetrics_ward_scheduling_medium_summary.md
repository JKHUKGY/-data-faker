Healthcare data consultancy Halcyon Health Analytics delivered an AI ward-scheduling POC called MaterniFlow for Cedar Ridge Women's Health, a regional obstetrics hospital chain; the dataset has 11 tables and about 500 rows, with core entities covering patient, ob_profile, admission, bed, labor_progress, vital_sign, medical_order, and alert, and supports 20 SQL queries used for shift-handoff headcounts, four-state bed availability, postpartum LOS prediction, high-risk blood-pressure trend alerts, and C-section resource scheduling — helping nurses and physicians confirm during the demo that the AI can give a clearer picture of ward status than the old CMS system; the data covers 50 patients, 16 admissions (15 current + 1 discharged), 20 rooms, and 32 beds.
