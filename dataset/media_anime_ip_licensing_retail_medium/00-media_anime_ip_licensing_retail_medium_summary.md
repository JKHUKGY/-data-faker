Ember & Ash Licensing Group is a fictional North American anime IP licensing agency headquartered in El Segundo, California, that licenses anime IP on behalf of rights holders to licensees who manufacture merchandise and pay royalties in return. The dataset includes 12 tables built around core entities such as anime IP titles, licensees, licensing contracts, SKUs, retail sell-through, streaming popularity, and royalty audits, and supports 20 SQL queries spanning aggregation, joins, CTEs, and window functions. The data supports business questions such as renewal priority ranking, royalty underreporting audits, popularity-to-sell-through lag analysis, unauthorized channel compliance detection, and minimum guarantee (MG) pacing tracking. The dataset contains 24 anime IP titles, 32 licensees, 117 licensing contracts, roughly 750 SKUs, and about 51,000 rows in total.
