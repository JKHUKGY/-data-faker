NorthArc Photonics Manufacturing is a fiber-optic connector maker in Hillsboro, Oregon, specializing in cable assembly built around the fusion splicing process; the dataset spans 18 tables and roughly 220,000 records, anchored by two core fact tables — splice_record (about 50,000 rows) and splice_attempt (about 55,000 rows) — and supports cost-attribution, yield-trend, cross-tab, and ROI-estimation queries; the data lets analysts trace concrete cost sinks such as electrode wear, night-shift quality decay, multi-core skill mismatches, over-quality on FTTH orders, ignored AI alerts, overdue equipment calibration, and recall risk from the bad batch MFG-2024-038; it covers 12 months of production data from 2025-06 through 2026-06 across 12 customers, 12 machines, and 30 operators, and is designed to help the CFO uncover at least $700K in annualized savings.
