CityPulse is an Austin-based Govtech SaaS company that provides a full lifecycle management platform for 311 non-emergency municipal service requests to 120 mid-sized city and county governments across North America, with annual ARR of roughly $50 million; the dataset contains 23 tables, with core entities service_requests, work_orders, model_predictions, and tenant_health_snapshots, supporting queries for SLA breach analysis, NLP classification accuracy evaluation, reopen and same-location recurrence detection, and city council complaint prediction; it can be used to diagnose business problems such as Other-category misrouting, resident-selected severity diverging from the request text, on-site quick-closes masking the true resolution rate, escalation-language early warning, and tenant-level model accuracy gaps; the data spans 36 months of about 1 million service requests, totaling roughly 10 million rows.
