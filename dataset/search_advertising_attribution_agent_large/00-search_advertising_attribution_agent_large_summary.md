This dataset portrays Lumina Reach Media Group, a North American media agency holding company whose 15 member agencies run paid search campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising for 80 advertisers spanning 10 IAB industries including retail, travel, and finance; the data spans 21 tables and roughly 570,000 rows, built around core entities advertiser, campaign, keyword, daily_stats, conversion, and attribution_path, paired with 20 SQL queries that support attribution comparison, budget pacing, and cross-engine efficiency analysis; it can be used to quantify how six attribution models, including last click, skew channel value rankings, identify campaigns constrained by budget or ranking, evaluate waste-management issues such as negative keyword gaps and broad match overspill, and measure ROAS and CPA performance by agency tier and industry; the account structure includes about 800 campaigns, 4,200 ad groups, and 42,000 keywords, with a fact layer of roughly 165,000 rows of daily performance data and 15,400 attribution touchpoints.
