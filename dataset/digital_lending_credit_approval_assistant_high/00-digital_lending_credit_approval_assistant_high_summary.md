Northpeak Lending is a digital consumer lending company headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, offering 7 active loan products including mortgages, auto loans, personal loans, debt consolidation loans, and HELOCs; the dataset consists of 18 tables (applicants, credit reports, loan applications, risk rules, approval decisions, fraud flags, and more) with over 18,000 rows, covering roughly 500 applicants and 800 loan applications along with their downstream rule evaluations, approval history, similar-case comparisons, and other details, and supports 20 SQL queries built on aggregation, multi-table joins, CTEs, and window functions; these queries can answer five core categories of business questions: approval health, product and channel profitability and geographic concentration, credit policy and risk rule effectiveness, fraud and compliance risk, and the trustworthiness of the AI approval assistant.
