This dataset portrays the customer success operations of NimbusScale, Inc., a mid-market North American cloud infrastructure provider, covering bundled subscriptions across compute, storage, network, and AI/ML inference along with an Enterprise/Business/Pro/Basic four-tier account structure; it consists of 11 tables (customer, subscription, usage_metrics, health_score, csm, csm_task, interaction_log, and others) totaling roughly 2,290 rows of data, and supports 20 SQL queries covering renewal risk alerts, health score and ticket correlation analysis, expansion opportunity identification, CSM workload assessment, and committed-versus-actual usage comparisons, serving analytical scenarios such as Text-to-SQL, customer 360, and renewal playbooks, at a scale of about 150 customers and 170 subscriptions.
