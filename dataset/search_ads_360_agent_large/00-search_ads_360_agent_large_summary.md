Lumenly Ads is a fictional cross-engine search advertising aggregation and management platform headquartered in Shanghai, positioned as a competitor to Google Search Ads 360 and Meta Ads Manager, and it charges clients a platform service fee of 8%-20% of their monthly ad spend; the dataset contains 21 tables, with advertiser, campaign, keyword, daily_stats, and attribution_path as its core entities, supporting Join, CTE, and window function queries for budget and impression share analysis, bid strategy performance, keyword quality diagnostics, search term mining, and multi-touch attribution model comparisons; it can be used to identify budget-constrained campaigns, diagnose high-spend/low-Quality-Score keywords, surface negative keyword and new keyword candidates, compare channel value across six attribution models, and assess advertiser renewal risk and agency service fee revenue; the data spans roughly 150 advertisers, 300 engine accounts, 1,550 campaigns, 8,000 ad groups, and 80,000 keywords, totaling about 1 million rows.
