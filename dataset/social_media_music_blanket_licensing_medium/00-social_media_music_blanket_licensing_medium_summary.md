ReelWave, Inc. is a fictional North American short-video social platform headquartered in San Francisco, whose Sounds team negotiates blanket licenses over master-recording catalogs with 20 record labels and aggregator distributors and runs the Creator Fund. The dataset contains 9 tables and roughly 24,900 rows, covering core entities such as labels, contracts, sounds (official tracks and UGC remixes), monthly catalog usage, rights-conflict flags, and weekly revenue splits, and supports SQL queries using joins, window functions, and CTEs. It can be used to investigate business questions such as drift in catalog usage share relative to contracted assumptions, SLA overruns and ad-revenue exposure from unauthorized-sample conflicts in UGC, systematic underpayment from the Creator Fund's weekly settlement of sounds that go viral across week boundaries, and whether MFN (most-favored-nation) clauses have been breached.
