NestMart is a fictional North American mid-to-upscale home goods e-commerce platform (about 70% self-operated as NestMart Original, about 30% third-party sellers); this dataset models the AI-first customer service ticket handling system it launched in November 2024, spanning 20 tables and roughly 25,700 rows in total, centered on raw_ticket (5,000 tickets) along with its Step1 intent recognition and Step3 policy decision AI outputs, paired with annotation_ground_truth human annotations (about 1,000 records) and 6 evaluation_run evaluation runs, supporting 20 SQL queries covering intent confusion matrices, R001–R007 business rule violations, Prompt version A/B comparisons, SLA and VIP prioritization, and annotation agreement (Cohen's Kappa); it can be used to quantify AI intent recognition accuracy (81%→90.5%) and rule violation rate (12%→3%), calculate the cost advantage of AI over human agents (under $0.01 per ticket versus about $4.69), and surface concrete business issues such as high-frequency refund fraud risk and discrepancies in refund authority between self-operated and third-party sellers.
