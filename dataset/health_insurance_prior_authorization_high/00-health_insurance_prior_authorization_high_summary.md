Meridian Health Plan is a fictional regional health insurance payer headquartered in Austin, Texas, selling HMO, PPO, and EPO policies across the Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum metal tiers on the ACA marketplace; this dataset models its Prior Authorization (PA) operations across 15 tables and roughly 8,200 rows, with core entities including plans, members, providers, pa_requests, pa_decisions, appeals, and claims, supporting SQL queries on turnaround time, approval rates, denial reason distribution, and appeal overturn rates; it can be used to analyze SLA breaches, whether approval rates are fair across metal tiers, denial code concentration, authorization-to-claim leakage, multi-level appeal overturns, and the effectiveness of automated review, with data anchored to the 12 months preceding the generation date, covering 400 members and about 700 PA requests.
